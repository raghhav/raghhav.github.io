Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

"Animal FaceOff Uploader" is a private application operated by the owner of the Animal Face-Off YouTube channel. It is used only to upload videos to that channel and to read the channel's own basic details.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and read the channel's name and video status. It does not access any other user's data, and no other users sign in to it.

How data is stored and shared

The only data kept is the OAuth login token, stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private repository. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel through its YouTube page.
